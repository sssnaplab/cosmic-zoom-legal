Cosmic Zoom Privacy Policy

Effective: November 18, 2026 · Developer: SnapLab

Cosmic Zoom (com.sssnaplab.cosmiczoom) is an educational app where children zoom from the edge of the universe down to quarks. The app does not collect any personal information.

Information we collect

None. No account, name, email, location, photos, contacts or device identifiers are requested or collected.

Stored on your device only

Only the discovered levels, the number of stars earned in missions, and whether the intro was shown are saved inside the app on the device. This never leaves the device and is removed when the app is uninstalled.

Internet, ads and tracking

The app has no internet permission and sends nothing to any server. There are no ads, analytics, tracking, in-app purchases or external links.

Permissions

The app requests no dangerous permissions (camera, microphone, location, storage, etc.).

Children

The app is designed for children and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
